Strategy Overview: Dynamic Pairs Trading in the AI Sector

This system implements a dynamic pairs trading strategy focused on the Artificial Intelligence sector. The model identifies pairs of stocks that exhibit long-term equilibrium (cointegration) and exploits short-term price divergences through a mean-reverting framework.

1. Parameter Calibration & Performance Hurdles

To determine the optimal thresholds for selection, a meta-optimization was executed on 2025 historical data. This process allowed for the identification of specific performance hurdles that most reliably predict out-of-sample stability.

The strategy filters candidate pairs using the following criteria:

  • Statistical Integrity: A Cointegration p-value < 0.05 (95% confidence).
  • Performance Requirements: A minimum Sharpe Ratio of 1.0 and positive annualized returns across both training and testing periods.
  • Reliability Metric: A Sharpe Stability ratio (Testing Sharpe / Training Sharpe) of at least 0.5 to mitigate the risk of overfitting.

2. Execution Logic & Risk Management

The strategy monitors the Z-score of the price spread, calculated over a 90-hour rolling window. This metric quantifies the number of standard deviations (σ) the current spread has drifted from its mean.

  • Entry Signal: A position is initiated when the spread deviates beyond 1.5σ.
  • Exit Signal: Positions are closed as the spread reverts to 0.5σ.
  • Protective Constraints: A Stop-Loss is triggered at 3.5σ. Additionally, new entries are blocked if the Z-score exceeds 2.5σ to prevent trading during extreme, non-reverting market shocks.
  • Transaction Costs: All performance metrics incorporate a 0.5% fee per trade.

3. Walk-Forward Simulation & Adaptive Re-optimization

Beginning January 1, 2026, the strategy operates as a live simulation using hourly data. To adapt to shifting correlations within the AI sector, the model employs a walk-forward approach with the following lookback structure:

  • Dual-Horizon Selection: Each pair must pass a one-year cointegration test to ensure a robust long-term statistical relationship, combined with a 90-day optimization window to capture recent market regimes.
  • Training & Testing: The 90-day window is split into a 60-day training period for parameter tuning and a 30-day testing period for out-of-sample validation.
  • Dynamic Hedging: An 18-day lookback is utilized to calculate the current hedge ratio and spread for active trading.
  • 60-Day Re-optimization: Every two months, the entire selection pipeline is re-run. The system prunes inactive pairs and rotates into new opportunities that meet both the long-term structural and short-term performance criteria. A pair is deactivated if it's no longer optimized and its last two months profit falls below a 3% threshold.
  • Capital Allocation: Total profit calculations assume an equal-weight investment across all active pairs at the commencement of each period.
